Continuous beams

Reactions, bending moments, shear forces and deflected shape of a straight continuous or statically indeterminate beam: simple supports, fixed supports, sliding fixed supports, springs, hinges and support settlements.

First-order linear elastic analysis; envelopes of the EN 1990 combinations with alternating variable loads; required reinforcement, resistance of the chosen bars and curtailment to EN 1992-1-1:2004, recommended values. The reinforcement remains to be designed and checked.
